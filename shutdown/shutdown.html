SariaSoftware Closure

As of January 4th 2025 the SariaSoftware GmbH will stop all it's operations.

As sorry as we are, the team came to the conclusion to shut down the Company.

To make it as short as possible to not waste anyones time, we do not have enought employees to deliver the quality we want to.

And since we are a fully private company with a team just concluding of friends and family we will not hire any new people.

We also had very bad experiences in the past regarding that topic.

We simply don't want to deliver less and less quality, so it ends here and now.

We have not decided if this is permanent yet or if we will continue our projects and operations on a later date.

But for now we will be shutting down, the name, the company will not be sold.

We will keep our copyrights, trademarks and licenses. To maybe continue operating in the future.

Thanks to all of our coworkers, customers and supporters.

Farewell, your SariaSoftware-Team.
